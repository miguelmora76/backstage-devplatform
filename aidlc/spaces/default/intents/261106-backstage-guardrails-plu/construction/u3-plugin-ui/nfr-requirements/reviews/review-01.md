### Summary

Every NFR1 to NFR7 has a detailed requirement with a test, and the security requirements agree with BR5.2, BR5.3, the audit actor filter and the C3 start address. Two Majors remain, within the READY allowance: the unsourced 6 s and 12 s limits conflict with NFR1.1's 5 s promise, and the NFR5.11 "no path in code" check cannot be met by a plugin that must call the start address. Fix both, and fill the smaller gaps, before Code Generation relies on them.
