### Summary

The design is coherent: a UI package needs no runtime infrastructure, and almost every security and performance control has a concrete `ui-*` job or an honest delegation to u5. Two Major items should be fixed at the gate: the lock-file allow-list check as written cannot work on yarn v1 or on `push` events and would break other units' pull requests, and the accessibility, coverage and u5-delegated NFRs are missing from the mapping.
