### Summary

The design is coverage-complete and keeps the screens following the backend's allowed actions. Two gaps need fixing before a developer can build Screen C and the change rows without guessing: how actions are shown when the backend returns a higher-precedence reason than the change state, and where the change title comes from, given that C1 has only `summary`. The remaining items are consistency fixes.
