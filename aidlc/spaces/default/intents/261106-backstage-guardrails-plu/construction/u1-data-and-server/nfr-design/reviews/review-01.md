### Summary

Three Major findings: the schema-before-handler design contradicts the BR3.3 check order and the one-line-per-call log rule, INTERNAL_ERROR has no home in the functional rules or contract, and traceability.json omits the observability and tech-stack NFRs. The rest of the design (single-event-loop writes, pre-ordered lists, repeatable generator, safe repeated writes) is sound. The Minor items clean up statements that tests cannot reach or that depend on u5 without saying so.
