### Summary

The NFR set covers all of NFR1–NFR7 and every cited ID resolves. The tech choices are consistent with the TypeScript, Backstage, PostgreSQL, local-only and tests-first constraints. The one Major gap is the 2,000-character reason cap and the INVALID_INPUT 400 behavior: they are new, but absent from the contract change list and from any rule that sets the check order. The Minor items cover identity edge cases, the demo-switch dependency on u4 and u5, and measurement precision.
