### Summary

The design is largely faithful to compose, the Dockerfiles and the person's answers (Kustomize, loopback port-forward, blocking live job with timing reported only), and its rights and mount tables, secret handling and the cluster guard hold up. Three Major gaps remain: the server-down scale sequence has no wait for the pod to go away or to come back, the probe timings are unspecified against the 1 s exec default and Backstage's start time, and the NFR5.13 live non-loopback refusal check (plus the repeated image checks) is not designed. These are one revision away from READY.
