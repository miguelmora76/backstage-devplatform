### Summary

The pattern is sound (two networks, one loopback port, tmpfs static copy, ci-ok), and the requirement IDs are all realised. Three gaps block it: no defined layout or start command for the app image (catalog and settings paths), a log scan that cannot see tokens inside compressed traces or browser-held tokens, and a PostgreSQL service that cannot start under the stated rights. Fix those and the minors, then it should be ready.
