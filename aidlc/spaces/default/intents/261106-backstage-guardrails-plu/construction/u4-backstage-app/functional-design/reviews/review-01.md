### Summary

The design stays within wiring and configuration, decides no business rule, and every acceptance criterion maps to a rule that matches the built plugins. The two Major gaps (the undeclared u1 dependency and the catalog's weakness to a non-default seed, and the undefined sign-in session lifetime) are closable by adding rules and do not need a redesign; with two Major findings the verdict stays READY.
