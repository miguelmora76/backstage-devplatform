### Summary

The four contracts cover every dependency edge, and the payloads match the component entities. The decision order and the timeouts are coherent, with the 10 s write timeout below the 30 s claim release. The two Major points need a decision at the gate. First, the "server down" rule is undefined and conflicts with the FR3.6 retry condition. Second, FR11.2 contradicts the Q2 decision that changes and audit reads keep working. Both are fixable by clarification rather than redesign, so the verdict is READY.
