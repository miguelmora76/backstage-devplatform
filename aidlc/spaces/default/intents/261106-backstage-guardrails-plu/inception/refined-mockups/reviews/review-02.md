### Summary

The revision settles the nine earlier findings. Every story with a screen has one, the three requirement changes are recorded with their origin, and the states, answers Q1 to Q10 and the four artifacts are largely consistent. Two Major points remain, both resolvable at construction: the backend identity and role source for the demo-user switch, and what happens to the stale-page refusal message after the reload. Engineering can start, so the verdict is READY.

### Suggestions

- Backstage `Table` enables sorting by default, so the mapping should say sorting is turned off, to match "No sorting" and the missing `aria-sort`.
- Say where focus lands after a stale-page reload, and after a user switch on Screen C when the current actions become disabled.
