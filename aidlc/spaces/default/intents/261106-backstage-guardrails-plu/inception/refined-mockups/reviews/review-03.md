### Suggestions

- The header switch changes a real sign-in identity while the page stays in place. Confirm in construction that the Backstage sign-in provider can swap identity without a reload; if not, say that the screen refreshes and keeps its place.
- The banner takes focus after Propose. A keyboard user then has to tab back to the row they were on; consider returning focus to the row button after the banner is read.
- A failed change that has been retried shows only one decision block. Say whether the block shows the latest attempt, or only the first decision.

### Summary

Every story (US1.1 to US5.2) and screen-relevant requirement is represented, all states are covered, and the artifacts agree with Q1 to Q14 and with P1 to P5. Two Major points remain (the string catalog is still incomplete, and the paging and caption are mislabelled as standard components), both with clear fixes, so engineering can start.
